02

Must-Haves — Capture Every Item必拍要求｜逐项完成

Stay on camera throughout全程真人露脸

Keep your face visible from the first frame through the end. For control or material close-ups, use a face-visible split-screen or keep yourself clearly in the same frame.从第一帧到结尾都保持真人露脸。按钮、结构和材质特写用露脸分屏，或让人物与细节在同一画面内都清楚可见。

True vertical, sharp details真正竖拍，细节清楚

Shoot native 9:16 at 1080p or higher, with clean speech and steady focus. Fill the frame, including split-screen shots; no black bars or blurred padding.以9:16原生竖拍，至少1080p，声音清晰，对焦稳定。分屏也填满竖屏，不加黑边或模糊填充。

Show the action and the result调节动作与使用结果都要拍

For each selected feature, record your hand operating the control, the visible change, and your seated use afterward. Keep one complete, normal-speed take of each operation.每个选定卖点都拍到：手部操作、部件变化、调节后的坐姿或使用状态。每项操作另保留一条正常速度的完整连续镜头。

Speak naturally, explain the advantage自然口播，说明产品优势

Use the SAY prompts in your own voice. Name the feature, explain why you use it, and describe your actual experience. Try the assembled chair before recording your verdict.SAY是表达提示，用自己的语言讲。说明这是什么功能、为什么要调、使用后有什么真实感受。先安装体验，再拍总结。

One real setting, two key benefits一个真实场景，两项核心卖点

Choose working, gaming, designing or editing as your main story. Film the two confirmed benefits clearly instead of racing through a long list of specifications.选择工作、游戏、设计或剪辑中的一个主场景，讲清品牌确认的两项核心卖点；不用在一条视频里堆满所有参数。

Preview, 5+ photos and usable files返视频、5+张照片和可下载原文件

Within 10 days of receipt, send the video preview and at least 5+ photos. Confirm advertising and website usage before filming, and deliver the agreed clean export and raw footage.收货后10天内提交视频预览与5+张照片。拍摄前确认广告、官网使用授权；最终提供约定的干净版与原始镜头。

Delivery & the Don’ts文件交付与注意事项

Do / deliver需要完成／交付

  1. Confirm marketing usage permission before filming.拍摄前确认品牌营销使用授权。
  2. Within 10 days of receiving the product, send an accessible preview link + 5+ high-resolution lifestyle photos.收货后10天内，提交可访问的视频预览链接＋5+张高清生活场景照片。
  3. Make requested revisions and send an updated preview. Wait for explicit approval.按审核意见修改，并重新提交预览；获得明确审核通过后再发布。
  4. Publish the approved video on the agreed platform (Instagram or TikTok) with the product link. For Instagram posts, accept the brand’s Collab invitation when included in the agreement.如合作包含账号发布，在约定的Instagram或TikTok发布审核通过的视频，并附产品链接；Instagram按合作约定接受品牌Collab邀请。
  5. Send the live post link when applicable and a working download link to the original, full-resolution approved video. A preview-only or social-media link is not the downloadable original file.提交帖子链接（如合作包含发布）及审核通过视频的完整高清原文件下载链接；仅可观看的预览或社媒链接不能代替原文件。

Don’t不要

  • Do not publish before approval.不要在审核通过前发布。
  • Do not submit landscape footage inside a portrait canvas.不要将横视频放入竖屏画布，不加黑边或模糊填充。
  • Do not hide your face during detail shots or demonstrations.细节拍摄及功能演示时不要隐藏人脸。
  • Do not skip the chair overview, adjustments, real use or product details.不要遗漏整椅展示、调节演示、真实使用及产品细节。
  • Do not promise medical outcomes or describe functions your sample does not have.不要承诺医疗效果，或描述实物不具备的功能。

5+ Lifestyle Photos — You + the Chair / Chair + Your Space返图5+张｜人物＋椅子／椅子＋空间

Deliver at least five high-resolution photos, including you using the chair, the chair in your space, product details and unboxing or assembly. Keep the person and chair clear in people-focused photos.至少交付5张高清照片，包含人物使用、空间全景、产品细节及开箱／组装。人物使用照中，人脸与椅子清楚可见。

Share downloadable photo files through Google Drive, Dropbox or a similar platform. The photos do not need to be posted on social media.通过Google Drive、Dropbox或类似平台提供照片下载链接。照片无须发布到社媒。
