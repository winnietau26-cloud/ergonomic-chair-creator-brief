02

Must-Haves — Capture Every Item必拍要求｜逐项完成

Stay on camera throughout全程真人露脸

Keep your face visible from the first frame through the end. For control or material close-ups, use a face-visible split-screen or keep yourself clearly in the same frame.从第一帧到结尾都保持真人露脸。按钮、结构和材质特写用露脸分屏，或让人物与细节在同一画面内都清楚可见。

True vertical, sharp details真正竖拍，细节清楚

Shoot native 9:16 at 1080p or higher, with clean speech and steady focus. Fill the frame, including split-screen shots; no black bars or blurred padding.以9:16原生竖拍，至少1080p，声音清晰，对焦稳定。分屏也填满竖屏，不加黑边或模糊填充。

Show the action and the result调节动作与使用结果都要拍

For each selected feature, record your hand operating the control, the visible change, and your seated use afterward. Keep one complete, normal-speed take of each operation.每个选定卖点都拍到：手部操作、部件变化、调节后的坐姿或使用状态。每项操作另保留一条正常速度的完整连续镜头。

Speak naturally, explain the advantage自然口播，说明产品优势

Use the SAY prompts in your own voice. Name the feature, explain why you use it, and describe your actual experience. Try the assembled chair before recording your verdict.SAY是表达提示，用自己的语言讲。说明这是什么功能、为什么要调、使用后有什么真实感受。先安装体验，再拍总结。

One real setting, two key benefits一个真实场景，两项核心卖点

Choose working, gaming, designing or editing as your main story. Film the two confirmed benefits clearly instead of racing through a long list of specifications.选择工作、游戏、设计或剪辑中的一个主场景，讲清品牌确认的两项核心卖点；不用在一条视频里堆满所有参数。

Preview, five photos and usable files返视频、5张照片和可下载原文件

Within 10 days of receipt, send the video preview and five photos. Confirm advertising and website usage before filming, and deliver the agreed clean export and raw footage.收货后10天内提交视频预览与5张照片。拍摄前确认广告、官网使用授权；最终提供约定的干净版与原始镜头。

Talking Points — Choose Your Opening开头与选题｜选择适合自己的表达

Use one opening in the main video. Record the other two as separate short clips with your face and the chair visible; deliver all three for ad editing. Keep the main story in one real setting.主视频选择一个开头，另外两个单独拍成短片，三个都露脸并让椅子入镜，供广告剪辑使用。主线围绕一个真实使用场景。

A · Setup storyA · 场景种草

“POV: your desk finally works for you.”“当你的桌面配置终于适合你自己。”

Begin seated in your actual setup. Turn toward the camera, then immediately show an adjustment.人物在真实桌面使用，自然转向镜头，再立即展示一个调节动作。

B · Direct-to-cameraB · 直接讲解

“Before you buy another chair, watch how I set this one up.”“换下一把椅子前，先看看我是怎么调这把的。”

Start with your face beside the chair, then cut to a face-visible control demonstration.脸和椅子同框直接讲，再进入露脸分屏的功能演示。

C · Immediate actionC · 立即操作

“These are the first two adjustments I make before I start work.”“开始工作前，我先调好这两个地方。”

Operate an actual control from the first frame with your face visible, then show the seated result.第一帧就操作真实调节部件，脸保持可见；接着拍调节后的使用状态。

Story options: gaming setup upgrade · a day working from home · editing or coding at your desk · switching from focus to a reading break. Pick the one you actually live; you do not need to shoot all four.主线选题：游戏桌搭升级、居家办公的一天、剪辑／编程的工作空间、工作到阅读休息的切换。选择本人真实场景，不要求一条视频拍齐四种。

Optional setup-switch opening: if you have your previous chair, record both chairs in the same workspace, with the same camera angle and your face visible. Cut from the old setup to the new one, then immediately demonstrate the benefit. One chair is enough if no genuine previous setup is available.可选：同机位桌搭升级开头。如果你保留原来的椅子，在同一桌面、同一机位分别拍旧椅与新椅，人物露脸；切换后马上进入功能演示。没有真实旧椅场景时，拍一把椅子即可。

“Same desk. New chair. Here’s what I changed for my workday.”“同一张桌子，换了新椅子。看看我为自己的工作日做了哪些调整。”
